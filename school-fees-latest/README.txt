SCHOOL, KIDZEE AND UNIVERSITY FEES: latest export
Built: 2026-10-12   (data compiled 2026-10-12)

FILES
 1_lucknow_all_schools.csv ........ 351 Lucknow schools (name, area, board, annual fee where shown). Fee shown for 189.
 2_kidzee_lucknow_centres.csv ..... 53 Kidzee centres in Lucknow with addresses. No fees: Kidzee centres are franchises and do not publish them.
 3_fees_detailed_lucknow_and_world.csv ... 41 detailed rows: top Lucknow schools, Kidzee/preschool references, Lucknow universities,
                                    universities worldwide, schools worldwide. INR columns use LIVE exchange rates from export time.

IMPORTANT
 * These are NOT official fee notices. They come from public aggregator pages and guides (links in each row). Schools change fees every year and
   usually charge extra for admission, transport, uniform, books and boarding. Confirm with the institution before deciding.
 * In file 1 the fee is ONE annual figure for a single class (10 or 12) as listed by Careers360, not a full fee schedule. Some figures look like
   data errors (for example INR 240, or INR 822,100): treat unusual values with care.
 * The only Lucknow Kidzee number found (INR 15000 a year at Gomti Nagar) is about four years old and unreliable.
 * Not found: verified Kidzee fees for any Lucknow centre; fees for the 162 schools that publish none; Lucknow University, Amity,
   Integral and BBD fees; London and New York school fees.
 * Files are UTF-8 with a byte-order mark so Excel shows the rupee sign correctly. Open with Excel, Google Sheets or LibreOffice.
